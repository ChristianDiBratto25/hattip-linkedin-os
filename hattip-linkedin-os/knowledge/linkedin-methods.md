# Strategic Tag

**Strategic Tag**  
---

**How It Works:**

Write out a process, framework, or idea that you would give to a specific person. In the post, tag them without forcing it.

**Why It Works:**

Humans are curious. If you get tagged in a post, you read the post. While demonstrating your competence to that one person, you're also putting it on display for everyone else to see. Plus, if they like it and do end up commenting, you get to reach their audience because of how LinkedIn pushes posts out.

# Industry Prediction

**Industry Prediction**  
---

**How It Works:**

State and justify a prediction you have about the future of your space. It should be non-obvious, insightful, and somewhat plausible.

**Why It Works:**

Similar to what makes the other methods work, this is prone to drive comments from people agreeing, disagreeing, or adding on to your take.

# Borrowed Authority Teardown

**Borrowed Authority Teardown**  
---

**How It Works:**

Pick a well-known brand or person in your space and tear down how they run the process or use the product that you / your company helps drive.

For example, we sell founder-led content services, so I would break down another founder social strategy to demonstrate my competence.

**Why It Works:**

Though you may not be well known, the person or brand you picked gets to lean on their name, logo, and likeness as a hook, but still gets to demonstrate your competence after the fact.

**Examples:**

[Nick Abraham](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/nick-abraham_ramp-is-worth-765b-and-has-been-non-stop-share-7270079475696967680-0NvP/)

# Tech Stack

**Tech Stack**  
---

**How It Works:**

Walk through the tech stack you use to run your business and deliver outcomes for customers. Importantly, tag the companies involved instead of using plain text. 

**Why It Works:**

For one, tech stacks in general tend to get saved by people who want to come back to them when they want to solve that problem. Perhaps even better, though, when you tag the companies, they tend to engage with the post, Again, pushing your post out to new audiences.

**Examples:**

[Tyler Leber](http://linkedin.com/posts/tyler-leber_every-software-we-use-while-growing-coconut-activity-7282425805391945728-mmuw)

# Tweet Screenshot

**Tweet Screenshot**  
---

**How It Works:**

1. Find a tweet related to what you do or what you sell.  
2. Screenshot it.  
3. Upload it as the media for your LinkedIn post.  
4. Write a post either agreeing with or disagreeing with the take.  
5. Justify your opinion.

**Why It Works:**

The tweet itself acts as a visual hook, plus your stance, whether agreeing or disagreeing, prompts people to comment (agreeing or disagreeing with you).

# News Headline

**News Headline**  
---

**How It Works:**

Find a news headline your ICP would care about. Screenshot it. Use it as a media for your LinkedIn posts and add some commentary. **The commentary must be valuable, or else this will flop.**

**Why It Works:**

Aside from making you a trusted resource to your ICP, you get to lean on the compelling news as a visual hook in the post.

# Bad Tactic Callout

**Bad Tactic Callout**  
---

**How It Works:**

Take a bad, scammy, or annoying tactic you see going around in your space. Screenshot an instance of it, use it as the media for your LinkedIn post, and call it out

**Why It Works:**

As is with what makes the rest of these work, these are very prone to drive comments of people agreeing and sharing their experience with the tactic.

# "If I Were"

**“If I Were”**  
---

**How It Works:**

Put yourself in your ICP's shoes and walk through the exact process end to end that you would use to get a desired result. This, of course, should tie back to whatever you do or sell. 

For example, we sell founder-led content services. My version of this is walking through how I would generate pipeline for a founder if I was writing their content.

**Why It Works:**

These are just highly saveable and shareable. You are speaking directly to your ICP and showing them that you are competent enough to solve a problem for them.

**Examples:**

[Christian Di Bratto](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7293252503226224643/)
