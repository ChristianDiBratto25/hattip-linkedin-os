# Funnel strategy

Default allocation is 40% top of funnel, 40% middle, 20% bottom. Change it when the offer, reputation, objective or audience justifies a change.

- TOF: relevant reach, recognizable interests, distinctive founder stories, industry reactions, company-building and broad opinions. Avoid attracting a huge irrelevant audience.
- MOF: teach how to achieve buyer outcomes; tactical processes, methods, teardowns, expert thinking, mistakes, education.
- BOF: demonstrate credible proof, examples, product or service use cases, objections and buying decisions. Never invent case studies.

Build 3 or 4 buckets per funnel stage. For every bucket record: precise buyer, positioning goal, why the author can speak about it, sample angles, appropriate format/media and proof available. A topic like "AI" is not a post idea. A genuine argument or process with specific stakes is.

Map each idea to one principal bucket and funnel stage. Favor diversity in angles, not arbitrary rotation of templates. A month's calendar should fit the agreed posting frequency. Generate 20 potential ideas but schedule only what the user can publish.
