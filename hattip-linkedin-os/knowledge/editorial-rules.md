# Editorial quality rules

Evaluate: idea strength, fit for buyer, hook clarity, package fit, specific useful information, logical flow, author voice, media, and accuracy. Diagnose upstream when copy editing won't help. Outputs: PASS, REVISE or RETHINK with the 1 to 3 highest-impact fixes and a revised draft when appropriate.

Prohibitions: em dashes; invented results, figures, facts and stories; "Here's the thing"; "Here's why"; "the reality is"; manufactured cliffhangers; fake reversals ("it's not X, it's Y"); rhetorical questions; repeated short fragments; marketing buzzwords; generic engagement CTAs; fake certainty; excessive bold and decorative formatting.

Use plain English, distinct observations, concrete examples and readable structure. Preserve the writer's meaning and strongest original phrases. Hooks may withhold a useful detail, but must make a specific, source-supported promise and orient a cold reader. Each paragraph should follow the last. Never conflate large engagement with commercial effectiveness.
