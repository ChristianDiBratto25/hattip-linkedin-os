# Reference use

Read `reference-posts.csv` and select links by method. Explain what to borrow structurally and what must be original. No fabricated links. If no good reference matches, state that instead of attaching an irrelevant example. LinkedIn posts may be unavailable or removed; do not claim current accessibility without checking. A reference example never proves that a format causes performance. Example posts are public posts we think illustrate each method well. Their authors aren't affiliated with this project, and listing a post doesn't mean Hat Tip wrote it or works with its author. Never describe a reference author as Hat Tip's client or as connected to Hat Tip.
