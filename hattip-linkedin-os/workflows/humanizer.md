# Humanizer

Use for: Edit every user-facing LinkedIn idea, strategy, draft and review for natural, grounded, human writing. Invoke during all writing workflows and standalone polishing.

Keep the author's actual voice and their strongest wording. Eliminate fake contrasts, rhetorical questions, em dashes, corporate buzzwords, artificial triads, repetitive sentence starts, fake suspense, generic encouragement, ornamental formatting and invented specificity. Preserve uncertainty, facts and details. Ensure each sentence logically follows the previous one. Give a cold reader adequate context. Do not announce this editing pass unless asked. For more detailed prohibitions see `knowledge/editorial-rules.md`.
