# Research

Use for: Identify current topics and credible industry discussions relevant to the LinkedIn ideal buyer. Use for timely research, industry conversations, news reactions or ICP trends.

Read the ICP and strategy before researching. When web research is available, look for recent primary sources and dates, verify the substance and ensure a meaningful author angle. Keep links and key factual context inside the idea description as needed, not in a separate source tracker. If browsing is unavailable, state it and ask for links or work from user-supplied context; do not invent a trend. Generate 3 to 5 specific, defensible angles with relevance and media suggestions. No automatic publication.
