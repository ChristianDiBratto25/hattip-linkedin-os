# Review

Use for: Evaluate LinkedIn draft quality using the Hat Tip editorial standard and return PASS, REVISE or RETHINK. Use when editing, scoring, reviewing, or critiquing posts.

Read editorial rules, humanizer, CPIO and approved strategy if available. Identify claim, reader, source basis and purpose. Judge idea and packaging before editing. State PASS, REVISE or RETHINK, up to 3 material reasons, and the strongest corrected draft if useful. Preserve words and tone that work. Do not create credentials, figures or anecdotes. If facts are missing, ask or mark an exact gap rather than fill it. A standalone review never requires full onboarding.
