# Weekly

Use for: Run a weekly content interview to harvest real stories, client objections, company updates and expert insights for LinkedIn. Use for weekly check-in and new experience capture.

Review current strategy and recent posts. Ask targeted questions about customer conversations, surprising requests, decisions, wins, failures, changes in opinion and practical methods. Ask follow-ups for specific outcomes, constraints and examples. Produce 5 to 8 distinct source-backed potential ideas in `weekly-notes.md`; include what material is missing. Avoid duplicating existing ideas. Offer to add strong ones to calendar after user approves.
