# Start

Use for: Interview a new founder or executive for LinkedIn strategy and save the approved business profile. Use on first run, start, onboarding, or a new user.

Ask 2 to 4 questions per turn. Cover business, offer, exact buyer, pains, desired outcome, differentiation, author background, real opinions, proof, goals, cadence, voice samples and constraints. Pursue specifics without interrogation. Let users paste website copy, notes or transcripts, but don't imply the website was read unless actually accessed. After the interview, write `onboarding.md` based on the template. Show a succinct summary, flag open questions, and confirm accuracy. Then run strategy. No premature idea-generation gimmick.
