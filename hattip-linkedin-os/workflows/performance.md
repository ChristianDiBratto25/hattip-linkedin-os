# Performance

Use for: Review a CSV of published LinkedIn posts, identify directional patterns and improve next-month content choices. Use for performance review, analytics or retrospective.

Ask for a CSV containing post date, text or ID, URL, impressions and engagement when available; business outcomes optional. Use the available columns. Describe sample size, outliers and limitations. Group directional trends by stage, bucket, topic, media and packaging when classification is defensible. Do not claim causes or conversions that are unmeasured. Suggest changes for next month; preserve approved strategy until user confirms. Save `performance-review.md`.
