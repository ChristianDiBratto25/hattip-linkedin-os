# Quality bar

Check whether a post offers a distinctive, credible idea with specific stakes, earns attention from a cold reader, supports the hook, and leaves the intended reader better informed. Strong execution often includes a clear process, verified numbers, direct examples, strong media, or a thoughtful reaction to a recognizable event.

Reference posts are examples of approaches, not templates to copy. Identify what is transferable before using one. User-supplied drafts are never rewritten to manufacture credentials or stronger certainty. Quality review should challenge a weak thesis before polishing sentences.

The original Hat Tip Quality Bar dataset contains third-party material and is not bundled wholesale in this public lead-magnet edition. The reference library is curated from the publicly linked LinkedIn Methods examples.
