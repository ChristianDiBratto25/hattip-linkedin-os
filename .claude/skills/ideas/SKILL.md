# Ideas

Read approved strategy, current calendar if any, reference-posts.csv, reference-policy, and methods as needed. Generate 20 DISTINCT ideas with a specific thesis or outcome, stage, bucket, reader relevance, suggested packaging, why now, required source detail, recommended media and one fitting real reference URL if available. Explain what to learn from that reference. Check repetition against posts and previous calendar. Mark ideas needing author evidence. Schedule the number corresponding to posting cadence, preserving 40/40/20 approximately over month. Never invent customer stories or claim references are verified live unless checked. Save `workspace/idea-bank.md` and `workspace/content-calendar.md`. Prioritize quality over filling a quota.
